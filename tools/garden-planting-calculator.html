  4. Use the result as a starting estimate when planning your garden.

🌿 Why Plant Spacing Matters

Plants need enough space for their roots, leaves and overall growth. Crowding plants together can make a growing area harder to manage, while excessive spacing may leave useful space unused.

The appropriate distance depends on the plant. Small herbs such as basil may need less space than larger vegetables or fruiting plants.

🌏 Sustainable Gardening

Growing some of your own herbs, vegetables or other useful plants can be a simple way to become more connected with nature. Even a balcony, container or small garden can provide an opportunity to observe plants and make thoughtful use of available space.

Start with what your space can comfortably support rather than trying to grow too much at once.

🌱 A Simple Reminder

This calculator provides an estimate rather than an exact planting plan. Real planting capacity can vary because of plant variety, mature plant size, sunlight, container shape, pathways and the space needed to move around your garden.
