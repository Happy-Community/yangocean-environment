☀️ How the Estimate Works

The calculator first estimates the potential solar capacity from the available area and panel efficiency. It then uses the average peak sun hours and a system-performance factor to estimate electricity generation.

The basic calculation is an approximation: Panel area × solar irradiance × panel efficiency × sunlight hours × system performance.

🏠 What Can Affect Solar Generation?

  • Roof or ground orientation
  • Roof angle and panel tilt
  • Shade from trees or nearby buildings
  • Local weather and seasonal sunlight
  • Panel temperature
  • Panel and inverter efficiency
  • Dirt, dust and maintenance
  • Available usable roof area

🌏 Why Solar Energy Matters

Solar energy uses sunlight to produce electricity without directly burning fossil fuels at the point of generation. For homes and other buildings, rooftop solar can be one way to explore the use of renewable energy.

However, solar panels are only one part of a sustainable energy approach. Reducing unnecessary electricity use and improving energy efficiency can also make a meaningful difference.

⚠️ Important Note

This calculator provides a simplified estimate for general awareness and planning. It is not a site assessment, engineering calculation, financial forecast or installation quotation.

Before installing a solar system, a qualified solar professional should assess the actual site, roof structure, shading, orientation, local solar conditions, electrical requirements and applicable regulations.
