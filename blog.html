🌱 About the Journal

Some reflections begin with something very small — a tree, a flower, a meal, a quiet moment or an ordinary experience.

From these observations, we can sometimes discover larger questions about how we live and how we relate to the world around us.

The purpose is not to provide perfect answers, but to encourage awareness, curiosity and thoughtful everyday choices.
