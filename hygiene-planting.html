🧼🌱 Hygiene & Planting

Clean habits and green actions create a healthier life and a more sustainable environment.

🌿 Why This Matters

A healthy lifestyle is built on two foundations: clean personal habits and connection with nature.

This page combines practical hygiene knowledge with real planting demonstrations.

🧼 Personal Hygiene

  • Wash hands regularly
  • Keep living space clean
  • Maintain personal cleanliness
  • Practice food hygiene

🏠 Clean Environment Habits

  • Reduce clutter
  • Dispose waste properly
  • Avoid unnecessary chemicals
  • Keep air and space clean

  • Place near sunlight
