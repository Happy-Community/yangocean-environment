A Moment of Mindfulness and Appreciation 🙏🌿

Sometimes, an ordinary encounter with another living being can bring a quiet sense of peace and gratitude.

At that moment, I stood quietly at the side of the road, giving way to an approaching car. My attention rested on a monitor lizard beside a tree within the monastery grounds.

There was no need to hurry, no need to do anything. I simply observed the lizard, appreciated its presence, and felt a quiet sense of peace and gratitude in my heart.

It was such an ordinary moment, yet it brought a gentle happiness.

Sometimes, peace is not found in doing more, but in being fully present with what is happening right now. When mindfulness meets appreciation, even a simple encounter with another living being can become a beautiful experience.

May we learn to slow down, appreciate the life around us, and live with mindfulness, patience, and loving-kindness towards all beings. 💚
