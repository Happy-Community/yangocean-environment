A Moment of Mindfulness and Appreciation 🙏🌿

Sometimes, an ordinary encounter with another living being can bring a quiet sense of peace and gratitude.

The Beauty of an Ordinary Moment

Sometimes, an unexpected encounter with nature can remind us to slow down and become more aware of the present moment. Even a brief experience beside a road can offer an opportunity to practise mindfulness and appreciation.

An Unexpected Encounter Beside the Road

During a visit to a monastery, I found myself standing beside a road when I noticed a monitor lizard near a tree. It had caught its prey and was holding it in its mouth. The lizard seemed to hesitate, as if deciding whether to cross the road.

At the same time, a car was approaching from the front. I became concerned about where I should go. I was also afraid that the lizard might cross towards me. For a moment, I felt uncertain about what to do.

Then I brought my attention back to my breathing. Rather than allowing my fear to take over, I tried to remain mindful of what was happening around me. I continued observing while staying aware of my own safety.

After a while, the lizard moved away, and the car slowly passed by my side. The situation ended peacefully and safely. What had begun as a moment of uncertainty became a quiet reminder of the value of mindfulness.

Mindfulness Does Not Mean We Never Feel Afraid

Mindfulness does not mean that we must never experience fear, worry, or uncertainty. These feelings can arise naturally when we face an unexpected situation.

What matters is learning to recognise our feelings without becoming completely carried away by them. Returning attention to the breath can help us pause, settle the mind, and see the situation more clearly.

Being mindful also means remaining aware of our surroundings and taking sensible action when safety is involved. Mindfulness is not about ignoring danger or forcing ourselves to remain still. It is about responding with greater awareness rather than reacting blindly.

A Simple Practice of Mindful Appreciation

We can bring this kind of awareness into ordinary moments throughout the day. When something unexpected happens, we can gently practise the following:

  1. Pause. Give yourself a moment before reacting, whenever it is safe to do so.
  2. Return to the breath. Notice the natural rhythm of breathing without forcing it.
  3. Observe. Become aware of what is happening around you, including people, animals, sounds, and your surroundings.
  4. Recognise your feelings. Acknowledge fear or worry without judging yourself for having them.
  5. Respond with care. Consider what is safe and appropriate for yourself and other living beings.
  6. Appreciate the moment. When the situation has passed, notice the relief and gratitude that may arise.

Appreciating Wildlife with Kindness and Respect

Wild animals are part of the natural world, each living according to its own needs and instincts. We may appreciate seeing them, but kindness also means respecting their space.

When observing wildlife, it is best to keep a respectful distance. We should avoid approaching, touching, feeding, or disturbing wild animals. Near roads, our own safety and the safety of others must come first.

Sometimes, simply observing quietly without interfering is already a meaningful way to appreciate another living being.

Finding Peace in Ordinary Moments

That brief encounter with the monitor lizard reminded me that mindfulness can be practised in the middle of everyday life, not only during formal meditation. A few mindful breaths can help us meet uncertainty with greater calm and awareness.

We cannot control every situation around us, but we can learn to notice our thoughts and feelings, stay attentive, and respond with patience and care.

May we learn to pause, breathe, and appreciate the precious moments around us. May we treat ourselves, other people, and all living beings with mindfulness, patience, and loving-kindness. 💚
