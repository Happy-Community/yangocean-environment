About Yangocean

Yangocean is a space for exploring simple ways of paying attention to life, health, nature and the world around us.

The website brings together reflections and practical ideas about mindfulness, eating well, connecting with nature, understanding our environment and making everyday choices more sustainable.

Some articles begin with a small observation — a tree, a drop of water, a plant, a meal or an ordinary moment — and use it as an opportunity to look more closely at the world around us.
